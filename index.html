▸ BEYOND ENGINEERING DEATH: TOPOLOGICAL RESILIENCE
We formalize the CNT lattice as a small‑world network where each carbon nanotube junction acts as a phase oscillator. The global synchronization emerges not from all-to-all coupling but via two synergistic mechanisms: (i) dust‑acoustic waves generating a macroscopic mean field \(\Psi(t)\) that is felt by every node (collective plasma hydrodynamics), and (ii) long‑range shortcuts intrinsic to small‑world topology (Watts–Strogatz) enabling fast phase front propagation. The distributed topological mode makes the system immune to local radiation damage — a single node degradation only increases local entropy without breaking the global invariant. The critical limit: the phase front velocity \(V_{\text{phase}}\) must exceed the degradation rate. Below the threshold, the network undergoes a second‑order phase transition into incoherence (therapeutic coma).

🏛️ Small‑World Topology & Fast Phase Front Propagation

The network is constructed as a Watts–Strogatz graph with \(N=56\) nodes (CNT junctions), initially arranged on a ring with 4 nearest neighbors. Rewiring probability \(p\) introduces long‑range links (magenta edges) that dramatically reduce the average path length \(\langle \ell \rangle\). This topology, combined with the global mean field, allows the synchronized mode to propagate faster than the characteristic degradation time. 💀 THE PRICE OF TOPOLOGICAL RESILIENCE: PHASE TRANSITION INTO “COMA”
When noise or degradation exceeds the critical bound, the mean field \(R\) collapses below \(R_{\text{crit}} \approx 0.38\) (depending on parameters). The system exhibits a second-order phase transition: local oscillators desynchronize, topological charge vanishes, and the plasma falls into a turbulent incoherent state. The simulation demonstrates this live: decrease \(K_{\text{loc}}\) or increase noise → watch R drop irreversibly unless mean field \(G_{\text{mf}}\) restores it. The small‑world topology widens the coherent regime but does not eliminate the fundamental kinetic limit.

📡 Architectural Verdict: Small‑World + Global Mean Field = Viable Topological Machine

The hybrid coupling scheme overcomes the fundamental "all-to-all vs locality" dilemma. The dust‑acoustic wave provides the macroscopic mean field \(\Psi\) that nudges every CNT toward global phase locking, while the small‑world shortcuts accelerate the information propagation. The topological invariant \(\mathcal{T}\) remains non-zero in the coherent state, protecting the distributed mode against local defects. The price is the critical velocity condition: if \(V_{\text{phase}}\) falls below \(L_{\text{corr}}/\tau_{\text{deg}}\) (e.g., due to plasma cooling or excessive noise), the system undergoes a second-order transition to phase turbulence — a reversible "therapeutic coma". Our interactive simulation demonstrates this edge of chaos: tune parameters and witness the order parameter collapse, then restore it via mean field coupling.
